# PPE & Occupational-Hygiene Standards — AS/NZS Reference

> **Purpose:** precise, citable AS/NZS standard numbers and edition years for a WA industrial/mining safety-advisor bot. Where a number/year could not be verified against an authoritative source it is flagged `[year/clause unverified]`. Edition status (Current / Superseded / Withdrawn / Redesignated) is stated per the Standards Australia and Standards New Zealand catalogues as of the compile date (2026-10-31).

> **Sources:** Standards Australia Store (store.standards.org.au), Standards New Zealand (standards.govt.nz), SAI Global / Intertek Inform, and SafeWork Australia / SafeWork NSW / WorkSafe VIC / WorkSafe QLD regulator guidance.

## 1. Respiratory protection

| Standard | Edition | Status | What it specifies |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AS/NZS 1715** — Selection, use and maintenance of respiratory protective equipment | **2009** | Current (supersedes AS/NZS 1715:1994) | Principles of respiratory protection; selection, use and maintenance of RPE; risk-management approach. Note: Standards Australia states it is intended to be replaced by the AS/NZS ISO series by 2030. |
| **AS/NZS 1716** — Respiratory protective devices | **2012** | Current (supersedes AS/NZS 1716:2003) | Product/manufacturing, performance, marking and testing of respirators (filters class P1/P2/P3, etc.). |
| **AS/NZS ISO 16975.3** — Fit-testing procedures | **2023** | Current | Identical adoption of ISO 16975-3:2017; qualitative and quantitative fit-testing of tight-fitting RPE, operator competence, record-keeping. Annual fit-testing recommended. |

- Fit testing is also mandated under **AS/NZS 1715:2009** (Appendix) — both methods (qualitative for half-face; quantitative for half/full-face and tight-fitting PAPR).
- Filters/eye-protector filter standards referenced: AS/NZS 1338.x (welding/UV/IR filters).

## 2. Hearing protection

| Standard | Edition | Status | What it specifies |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AS/NZS 1269.1** — Occupational noise management, Part 1: Measurement and assessment of noise immission and exposure | **2005** | Current | Method for measuring/assessing noise exposure; defines the **LAeq,8h** used by the regulatory exposure standard. |
| **AS/NZS 1269.3** — Occupational noise management, Part 3: Hearing protector program | **2005** (reconfirmed 2016) | Current | Selection, use, maintenance, training for a hearing-protector program; Class 1–5 method. |
| **AS/NZS 1270** — Acoustics — Hearing protectors | **2002** (reconfirmed 2014) | Current | Design, material and performance (attenuation) requirements; SLC80 rating and Class marking on packaging. |

- **The 85 dB(A) 8-hour exposure standard is a regulatory limit, not a standard number.** It is defined in the Model WHS Regulations (and mirrored in WA WHS Regulations) as: **LAeq,8h = 85 dB(A)** and peak **LC,peak = 140 dB(C)**, with LAeq,8h determined in accordance with AS/NZS 1269.1. Do not cite "85 dB(A)" as an AS/NZS number.
- Note: US "NRR" rating is **not** acceptable in Australia; use SLC80/Class per AS/NZS 1270.

|---|---|---|---|
| **AS/NZS 4602.1** — High visibility safety garments, Part 1: Garments for high risk applications | **2011** (Amd 1:2016) | Current in NZ; **superseded in AU** by AS 4602.1:2024 | Visual/conspicuity requirements (design, background material area, retroreflective placement) for high-visibility garments. |

- **4602.1 note:** the 2011 edition was redesignated AS/NZS → AS by Amendment 2 (Feb 2020). Australia has since issued AS 4602.1:2024; New Zealand continues to recognise AS/NZS 4602.1:2011. Refer to both depending on jurisdiction.

| **AS/NZS 2161.2** — General requirements and test methods | **2005** (reconfirmed 2016) | Current | General glove construction, innocuousness, comfort, marking (adopts EN 420:2003). |
